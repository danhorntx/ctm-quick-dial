CTM Quick Dial privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

CTM Quick Dial is a Chrome extension that lets you place calls through your own CallTrackingMetrics (CTM) account by clicking phone links on web pages. It is not made by or affiliated with CallTrackingMetrics.

What the extension handles

  • Your CTM login. The extension uses the CTM login already in your browser to get a short-lived phone session from CTM (app.ctm.com) and hands it to CTM's own phone, which runs inside the extension. This session is held in memory only. It is never stored, logged or sent anywhere except to CTM.
  • Phone numbers you call. When you click a phone link, the number (and a name, if the page supplies one) is passed to CTM's phone to place the call. Your last 15 calls are kept in Chrome's storage on your computer so you can redial them. You can clear them at any time from the toolbar button.
  • Settings. Your settings (dial immediately, default country, sites where the extension is off) are saved with Chrome's sync storage, so they follow your Chrome profile.
  • Troubleshooting log. A short technical log is kept on your computer to help fix problems. It never contains passwords or tokens, and phone numbers in it are masked.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not read or collect the content of the pages you visit. It only notices clicks on phone links.
  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any server other than CallTrackingMetrics.
  • It does not sell or share data, use it for advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

CallTrackingMetrics

Calls are placed and recorded by CallTrackingMetrics under your account. How CTM handles call data is covered by your agreement with CTM and its privacy policy.
